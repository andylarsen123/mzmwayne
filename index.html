Zoning laws dictate what can be built. 

Covering most of the land across the state of Michigan, the laws found in local zoning ordinances have a tremendous impact on our economy, environment, and society. But often, they are hard find, understand, and interpret. That’s why we are collaborating with local, state, and national stakeholders to create the Michigan Zoning MAP: a user-friendly map to visualize how housing is permitted by zoning ordinances across the state.

Rising housing costs and a mismatch between Michigan’s housing stock and its households have sparked local, regional, and statewide interest in understanding how, exactly, zoning is impacting housing--especially across the many communities that make up a single housing system. Perhaps surprisingly, no formal assessment of this is possible right now. No official record tells us how many communities have zoning ordinances, much less what those ordinances say; there's no report on the impact of one ordinance, much less the collective.
